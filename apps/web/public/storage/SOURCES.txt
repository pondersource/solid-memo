The Recipes, Movies and Flashcards modules own scope storage. Flashcards
preserves native Solid documents and retained adapter documents at their original
URLs; native Solid sharing uses the Pod access-control implementation. Internal RDF identifiers are not public links.
Public sharing copies only selected documents; private originals stay private.
Third-party license notices are retained in the sibling *.LICENSE.txt files.

Google/Dropbox callback: this app's HTTPS origin without a trailing slash.
Google also needs the app origin registered as a JavaScript origin.
Google and Dropbox return to the home route; Solid restores the previous route.
OneDrive also needs this app's callback registered in its public SPA registration.
LWS OpenID requires a chosen production issuer, public client and registered callback.
Acceptance tests supply a disposable client for their own CSS-LWS issuer.
